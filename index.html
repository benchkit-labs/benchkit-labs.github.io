How we build

The same four rules, every time

There's no house framework, no shared runtime, no telemetry pipeline. Just a set of constraints we hold every tool to.

Readable

If you can't open the source and understand it in one sitting, it's too big. No build steps to reverse-engineer, no minified bundles — what you read is what runs.

Private by default

No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no calling home. Web tools run entirely in your browser; desktop tools run entirely on your machine. Your data stays yours.

Free & libre

Everything is MIT-licensed and zero-cost. Fork it, self-host it, strip our name off and ship your own. There's nothing to monetize and nothing to lock you in.

Small on purpose

One tool, one job. We'd rather ship three sharp utilities than one bloated suite. When a tool is done, it's done — and it keeps working.

Built in the open.

Every Benchkit Labs tool is free, libre, and yours to read, fork, and self-host. Browse the source, file an issue, or just take what you need.
